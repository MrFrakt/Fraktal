## 1. Why running against the loaded build is valid

The declaration is one commit ahead of the controller (`0f96173`, the
ModelRequest write-surface closure). That change is **access only**: generated
logic, UDTs and AOI definitions are byte-identical, and what moves is one tag's
`ExternalAccess` plus the published field list. The parity harness fingerprints
on schema version, task period and scan liveness, not on `ContentHash`, so it
measures the same program either way — and because the program is literally the
same, a result gathered here transfers to the regenerated build.

Stated so the next reader does not have to re-derive it: **this is not a
result taken on a stale build and quietly generalised.** The two builds differ
in what a CIP client may write, not in what the controller does.
